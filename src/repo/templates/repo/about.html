HLArepo is the public repository of HLA allele and haplotype curations published by the Clinical Genome Resource (ClinGen). Each curation classifies the relationship between an HLA allele or haplotype and a disease, based on the published evidence.

Expert curators create these curations in the HLA Curation Interface (HCI). A curation appears in HLArepo once it has been approved and published. HLArepo keeps the full history of each published curation, and all of its data is free to browse and download.

HLArepo and the HCI are developed and maintained by the Stanford University contingent of the Clinical Genome Resource project.
