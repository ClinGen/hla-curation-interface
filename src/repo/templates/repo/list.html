HLArepo

The ClinGen repository of published HLA allele and haplotype disease curations.

Each curation in HLArepo classifies the relationship between an HLA allele or haplotype and a disease, based on evidence from the published literature. ClinGen expert curators create and review curations in the HLA Curation Interface, and a curation appears here once it has been approved and published.

Every published curation keeps its full change history, and all of the data is free to browse, search, and download.
